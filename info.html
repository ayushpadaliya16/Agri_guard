About Agri-Guard / Agri-Guard के बारे में

🎯 Mission / उद्देश्य

Protect Indian farmers from pesticide poisoning by providing instant, AI-powered safety information in their native language. भारतीय किसानों को कीटनाशक विषाक्तता से बचाना।

🤖 Technology / तकनीक

Uses OCR (Optical Character Recognition) to read pesticide labels and AI (Groq LLaMA) to analyze chemicals and provide safety guidance. ओसीआर और एआई तकनीक का उपयोग।

📶 Offline Mode / ऑफलाइन मोड

Works without internet using local Tesseract OCR and an offline database of 500+ chemicals. No data is stored or shared. इंटरनेट के बिना भी काम करता है।

🔒 Privacy / गोपनीयता

No personal data is collected. Images are processed locally and never stored on any server. कोई व्यक्तिगत डेटा एकत्र नहीं किया जाता।

Pesticide Safety Rules / कीटनाशक सुरक्षा नियम

1

Always Read the Label / हमेशा लेबल पढ़ें

Before using any pesticide, read the entire label carefully. Follow all instructions exactly as written. Use only for the crops and pests listed on the label.

2

Wear Protective Gear / सुरक्षा उपकरण पहनें

Always wear gloves, mask, goggles, and full-body clothing when handling pesticides. Never eat, drink, or smoke while spraying. दस्ताने, मास्क और चश्मा पहनें।

3

Check Weather Conditions / मौसम जांचें

Never spray during rain, strong wind, or extreme heat. Spray early morning or late evening. Avoid spraying near water bodies. बारिश या तेज हवा में छिड़काव न करें।

4

Correct Dosage / सही मात्रा

Use only the recommended dose. More is not better — excess pesticide harms crops, soil, and your health. Use the dosage calculator in the app. अधिक मात्रा हानिकारक है।

5

Safe Storage & Disposal / सुरक्षित भंडारण

Store pesticides in original containers, locked away from children and food. Never pour leftover pesticide into water sources. Triple-rinse empty containers before disposal.

🏥 First Aid for Pesticide Poisoning / कीटनाशक विषाक्तता में प्रथमोपचार

If swallowed: Do NOT induce vomiting. Call 112 immediately. Show the pesticide label to the doctor.

If on skin: Remove contaminated clothing. Wash skin with soap and water for 15 minutes.

If in eyes: Flush with clean water for 15–20 minutes. Seek medical help immediately.

If inhaled: Move to fresh air immediately. If not breathing, give artificial respiration. Call 112.
